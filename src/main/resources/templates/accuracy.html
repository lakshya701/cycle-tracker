How the test works

For every cycle you've logged, each method predicts its length using only the cycles before it — just as if it were predicting the future at that time. We then measure how many days off it was. The average of those errors is the mean absolute error; lower is better.

Testing on data a method hasn't seen avoids fooling ourselves: a method can look perfect on data it has already been fitted to, but what matters is how well it predicts the next cycle.
